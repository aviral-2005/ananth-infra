## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking, high-reliability infrastructure aesthetic rooted in ecological stewardship and engineered longevity. It blends enterprise-grade reliability with contemporary architectural elegance. 

### Design Philosophy
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Modern Clean Architecture with subtle Indian ecological elegance—leveraging profound botanical forest greens, crystalline sunlight accents, and crisp technical structure.
- **Tone & Mood:** Authoritative, high-conviction, serene, and engineered. It balances industrial strength (infrastructure, commercial rooftops, utility-scale deployments) with residential approachable luxury.
- **Visual Discipline:** Expansive whitespace, intentional asymmetry, razor-sharp typographic scale, and structural wirelines that communicate precision engineering. High-converting interaction touchpoints deploy high-visibility sun-gold and communicative green signals.

## Colors

The color system establishes a balanced harmony between architectural strength, natural energy generation, and utility trust.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Deep Forest Green (`#14532D`):** Anchors high-priority headers, dominant corporate surfaces, primary button containers, and critical structural anchors. Represents permanence and enterprise capacity.
- **Secondary Vibrant Green (`#22A447`):** Represents kinetic green power, generation metrics, affirmative validation states, and high-conversion direct actions (such as instantaneous WhatsApp and advisory dispatch).
- **Tertiary Solar Gold (`#FACC15`):** Reserved for solar irradiance cues, ROI highlights, premium milestone tags, and critical transactional callout buttons.
- **Surface / Light Green Tint (`#F0FDF4`):** Soft environmental canvas layer for analytical cards, savings calculators, and low-contrast backdrops.
- **Dark Slate Text (`#17251C`):** High-contrast base neutral for all foundational typography and primary interfaces, softer than pure black.
- **Subdued Slate Text (`#64748B`):** Secondary informational copy, metadata, metric subtitles, and structural labels.
- **Border Neutral (`#E2E8F0`):** Fine architectural hairline delineation for cards, data tables, and spatial separations.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas and elevated card surfaces.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Manrope** for authoritative, geometric display surfaces with **Inter** for ultra-clean readability in data telemetry, financial solar calculations, and technical schematics.

- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric and contemporary, with tightly kerned tracking on large scales to give infrastructure claims confidence and impact.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Highly legible across varied viewports, providing tabular clarity in generation metrics, kilowatt-hour savings calculations, and contract summaries.
- **Numerical Metric Displays:** When displaying ROI numbers, generation stats (kW, MW, units), apply semi-bold to bold weights with tabular lining figures.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid governs desktop and tablet presentations, consolidating into a single-column structured view on mobile devices.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout, 24px gutters, generous section margins (min 48px), max-width bounded at 1280px for standard views and 1440px for broad dashboard layouts.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, 32px lateral margins.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 20px margins.

### Spacing Principles
- **Atmospheric Density:** Clean, breathable vertical cadences. Content sections use wide macro-spacing (`space-xl` and higher) to communicate scale and calm expertise.
- **Component Compactness:** Micro-spacing inside interactive cards and calculation modules remains compact and disciplined using `space-xs`, `space-sm`, and `space-md` to preserve high data density.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system shuns heavy, artificial shadows in favor of a crisp architectural approach utilizing low-contrast hairline outlines, tonal depth, and delicate, sun-diffused ambient glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Solar Mist Tint (`#F0FDF4`). No shadow, framed cleanly by hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards / Inputs):** Surfaces sit on white base canvas with an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements / Popovers):** Used for savings calculation flyouts, solar project previews, and navigation menus: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Action Sticky Bars / Modals):** High-impact booking overlays: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(23, 37, 28, 0.12)`.
- **Energy Glow (Accents):** Selected solar savings cards and conversion CTAs may project a subtle 12px blur halo tinted with `#FACC15` (15% opacity) or `#22A447` (20% opacity) on active states.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting high-precision solar module fabrication—precise, geometric, but humanized with curved corners.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, badges, and smaller data cards.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Applied to primary content panels, project case study cards, and calculator containers.
- **Extra Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Applied to full-width feature hero banners and major modal containers.
- **Full Radius (Pill):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, micro tags, and specialized floating conversion pills (e.g., "Request Rooftop Audit").

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Corporate CTA:** Solid Forest Green (`#14532D`) background, pure white text, bold `Inter` label. On hover, smooth transition to deep emerald tint with `0 4px 12px rgba(20, 83, 45, 0.2)`.
- **Direct Conversion / WhatsApp CTA:** Vibrant Green (`#22A447`) background with pure white typography and integrated WhatsApp icon. On hover, brightness increase and subtle upward translate (-1px).
- **Solar Highlighting CTA:** Solar Gold (`#FACC15`) fill with Dark Slate (`#17251C`) text. Deployed in calculation outcomes, financial savings triggers, and key quote requests.
- **Ghost / Outline:** Transparent background, `1px solid #14532D` or `#E2E8F0`, with `#14532D` text.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Standard Module Card:** Pure White background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border, 16px radius, Level 1 shadow. Generous 24px inner padding.
- **Solar Analytics Card:** Background tinted with Light Green (`#F0FDF4`), with a thin vertical structural accent bar (4px) in Vibrant Green (`#22A447`) along the left perimeter.
- **Case Study / Infrastructure Preview:** Rich full-bleed photographic cards of commercial installations capped with an inner gradient overlay that transitions seamlessly into Dark Slate (`#17251C`) for text legibility.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** 48px height, 8px radius, white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Label positioned above in Slate Subdued (`#64748B`). Active/Focus states transition border to Vibrant Green (`#22A447`) with a 3px soft green glow ring (`rgba(34, 164, 71, 0.15)`).
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes:** Custom 20px controls with a rounded base. When checked, filled with Vibrant Green (`#22A447`) bearing a white geometric check or inner disc.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicators:** Pill-shaped, 6px vertical padding, 12px horizontal padding.
  - *Tier 1 (Commissioned/Active):* `#F0FDF4` background, `#14532D` text, 1px border `#22A447` at 30% opacity.
  - *Tier 2 (Subsidy Eligible / Premium):* `#FEFCE8` background, `#854D0E` text, subtle `#FACC15` highlight.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Solar Generation & Savings Bar:** Clean linear progress component visualizing grid offset percentage. Track background `#E2E8F0`, fill dynamic gradient running from `#22A447` to `#FACC15`.
- **Rooftop Calculator Module:** Multi-step stepped input container using clean card tiers, tabular numerical dials, and instantaneous real-time cost-benefit counters.
